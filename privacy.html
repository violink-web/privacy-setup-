Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Bay Brain Trivia is designed for local, in-person trivia games.

Information we handle

The app lets players enter a display name and submit team answers. Your display name is stored on your device. During a local game, display names, team membership, answers, scores, and leaderboard updates are shared only with nearby devices in the same game session.

How information is used

This information is used only to run the active trivia game. Bay Brain Trivia does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud storage, or third-party data collection services.

Local network access

Bay Brain Trivia uses your local network to discover nearby game hosts, team captains, and players. Local-network messages are encrypted by Apple’s system networking services when devices connect.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Game information is not sent to a remote server. Display-name data remains on your device until you remove it by deleting the app.

Your choices

You can decline local-network permission in Settings. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting Bay Brain Trivia from your device.
